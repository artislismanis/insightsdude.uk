Continuous delivery turns releases from a rare, high-stakes event into a routine, boring one. Smaller batches mean smaller blast radius, faster feedback, and easier rollbacks.

## Why it works

Shorter cycles surface integration problems while the change is still fresh in everyone's head, so the cost of fixing them stays low.
